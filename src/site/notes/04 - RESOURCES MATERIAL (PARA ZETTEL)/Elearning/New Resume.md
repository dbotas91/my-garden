## ==Objective==

💡

==The objective should focus on== ==what you can offer the company====, rather than what the company can offer you.==

💡

==This is not the time to be shy.== ==Sell yourself expressively.== ==Explain the value you will bring to this role.==

💡

==Repeat yourself.== ==Don’t be afraid to mention key points of your work twice (or more). It’s great for SEO, too.==

💡

==Do not just list the tools== ==you’ve worked with. Explain how you use each of them separately.==

⭐
